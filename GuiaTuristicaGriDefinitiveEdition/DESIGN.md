## Brand & Style

This design system draws from the tactile solemnity of Castilian historical villas, old stone arcades, monastic chronicles, and embossed leather cartography. It is tailored for cultural tourists, heritage explorers, and architectural pilgrims seeking an immersive, slow-travel companion.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Medieval Romanticism** with high-end **Editorial Restraint**:
- Visual metaphors rely on parchment planes, chiselled limestone reliefs, wrought-iron hardware, and heraldic filigree.
- Tactility is achieved through organic warm earth undertones, subtle deckled edges, ornamental structural borders, and etched framing lines.
- The tone avoids theme-park parody; it balances authentic historical weight with effortless, modern editorial utility and crisp typographic hierarchy.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture replicates authentic natural materials of medieval Spain: limestone walls, aged calfskin vellum, old church gold leaf, wrought iron, and kiln-fired clay.

### Palette Composition
- **Primary (`#7A2E1E` - Almagre & Cuero Repujado):** A deep, oxidized iron red and cured cordovan leather used for primary actions, heraldic highlights, and focal interactive anchors.
- **Secondary (`#B8860B` - Oro Viejo & Retablo):** An aged, tarnished antique gold reflecting altar leafing and historical signposts, used for badges, star ratings, and ornamental dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#2B3A42` - Pizarra & Hierro Forjado):** Cold iron-slate, bringing grounding contrast to headers, structural rules, and dense historical metadata.
- **Neutral (`#F4EBD9` - Pergamino & Caliza):** The base canvas tone of warm, unbleached parchment and weathered stone.

### Dark Mode Semantic Shifts
When toggled to dark mode:
- Canvas shifts to deep charcoal slate (`#181614` - Pizarra de Muralla) with ash undertones.
- Surfaces adopt darkened smoked-wood tones (`#23201D`).
- Primary actions shift to molten torch embers (`#C24D33`).
- Accent lines and iconography adopt antique gilded brass (`#D4AF37`) for legibility against nocturnal masonry.

## Typography

Typography functions as the primary vehicle of historical immersion, balancing monastic warmth with sharp legibility.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the weight of monumental stone carvings and royal decrees. Display scales feature small-caps treatments for sub-headers and heraldic inscriptions.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Literata):** Engineered for prolonged, effortless reading of legends, itineraries, and architectural histories, maintaining bookish clarity across daylight glare or torchlit night modes.
- **Labels, Metadata & Signage (EB Garamond):** Rendered with deliberate tracking (`0.05em` - `0.08em`) to mirror archival annotations, map legends, and engraved ledger markers.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an editorial manuscript framework inspired by medieval illuminated folios and classical cartography:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`margin-desktop: 3.5rem`, `gutter-desktop: 2rem`), collapsing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Folio Margins:** Generous framing mimics wide parchment book margins, giving architectural engravings and narrative prose space to breathe.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile (< 640px)`: Vertical scrolling chronicle layout with anchored navigation.
  - `Tablet (640px - 1024px)`: Asymmetric two-column layouts pairing maps/monuments with historic commentary.
  - `Desktop (> 1024px)`: Wide-canvas layout with flanked illuminated drop-caps, ornamental marginalia, and multi-layered route itineraries.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than synthetic Gaussian blurs, this design system communicates depth through **mineral stratification, physical debossing, and aged layered surfaces**:

1. **Stratified Parchment Tiers:**
   - Base Layer: Textured parchment tone (`#F4EBD9`).
   - Raised Cards: Sifted bone-white vellum (`#FAF5EC`) framed with crisp 1px borders resembling carved masonry joints (`#D8CAAF`).
   - Recessed Wells: Inset surfaces (search bars, timeline slots) use soft inner borders and an earth undertone (`#ECE1CA`) indicating pressed leather or etched stone.
2. **Shadow Character:**
   - Ambient shadows are tinted warm sepia rather than neutral grey (`rgba(58, 38, 20, 0.08)` to `rgba(58, 38, 20, 0.16)`).
   - Offsets remain shallow (1px to 4px) to simulate flat, stacked sheepskin pages or chiselled flagstones rather than floating planes.
3. **Ornamental Filigree & Hairlines:**
   - Double-line rules (a 1px solid stroke enclosed by subtle 0.5px auxiliary lines) replace drop shadows to structure page sections and guide the traveler's eye.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft, hand-chiselled stone profile (`roundedness: 1`)**:
- Corners mimic traditional masoned blocks or hand-cut leather patches rather than plastic geometry.
- Base interactive elements (buttons, inputs, cards) receive a restrained `0.25rem` radius.
- Heraldic badges, architectural seals, and historical milestone shields use chamfered corners or clipped octagonal geometries via CSS polygon masks.
- Ornamental border frames feature subtle corner flourishes or diamond rosettes (`✦`) at structural intersections.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Heraldic Button:** Rich cordovan red fill (`#7A2E1E`), antique gold typography (`#F4EBD9`), 1px chiselled border (`#5A1F13`), finished with a `0.25rem` radius. Hover states darken to cured leather (`#5E1F12`) with a faint gilded glow.
- **Secondary Relic Button:** Parchment surface with a 1px slate-iron border (`#2B3A42`), uppercase tracked text in `EB Garamond`, and corner notch accents.
- **Text / Anchor Links:** Terracotta brown with an ornamental dotted underline (`border-bottom: 1px dotted #B8860B`).

### Heraldic Badges & Chips
- Designed as medieval guild marks and route seals.
- Light gold and limestone background with a 1px border in aged bronze.
- Prefixed by heraldic symbology (e.g., keys, fortress towers, acorns, cross flory).

### Itinerary & Historical Monument Cards
- Card containers mimic framed manuscript plates: clean parchment background (`#FAF5EC`), framed in thin antique iron (`#D8CAAF`), accompanied by a top decorative ornamental header bar.
- Media elements within cards feature a slight sepia filter and etched vignettes around photography.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Inset parchment styling with a debossed inner contour (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 2px rgba(58, 38, 20, 0.12)`).
- Placeholder text in italicized `Literata` imitating hand-scribed calligraphic notes.
- Focus indicator consists of an antique gold outer hairline border (`#B8860B`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Styled after iron seals and forged nails.
- Checkboxes use squared iron boundaries; checked states display a stylized cross mark (`✕`) in warm crimson.
- Radio buttons feature concentric medieval coin medallions.

### Lists & Chronological Timelines
- Timeline tracks rendered as iron chains or weathered mortar lines with stone-marker milestones (`✦` and heraldic crest nodes).
- List entries divided by delicate, tapered gold fillets (`border-image` horizontal rules).

### Architectural Audio Guide Player & Cartography
- Floating audio pill designed like an embossed leather pocket watch or reliquary, holding transport controls framed in aged brass and dark iron accents.
